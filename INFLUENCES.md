# INFLUENCES

the loose canon for `[carburetor]` specifically. read these to understand what we are, by knowing what we are next to.

## kindred-spirit projects

- **Beepy / Beepberry** — sqfmi, eric migicovsky, 2023. open-source pocket linux with BlackBerry Q10 + Sharp Memory LCD + Pi Zero. closest existing analog of mk i compute. <https://beepberry.sqfmi.com>
- **MNT Reform** — mnt research, berlin. fully open ARM laptop, CNC aluminum, printed paper handbook. the architectural ancestor of our docs discipline. <https://mntre.com/reform>
- **Pocket Reform** — mnt's handheld. same DNA, our size class.
- **Playdate** — panic + teenage engineering, 2022. yellow handheld with a crank, 1-bit reflective Sharp memory LCD, weekly "season" game drops. the crank is the reason we have one too.
- **Light Phone II/III** — joe hollier + kaiwei tang. e-ink dumb phone, "designed to be used as little as possible." coined "going light" as a verb.
- **Punkt MP02** — petter neby, designed by jasper morrison. a phone designed by someone who designs everything.
- **Framework Laptop** — hot-swappable expansion-card I/O. precedent for our editions framework.
- **Notkia / Linkia** — sudomaker / reimu (china-diaspora). replacement PCB for nokia 1680 shells. china-flavored open-hardware lineage we sit inside.
- **Pebble reborn** — migicovsky, core devices, 2025. PebbleOS open-sourced by google jan 2025. "a labour of love rather than a startup trying to sell millions of watches."
- **PinePhone / Librem 5** — linux phones with hardware kill-switches. precedent for our brass mil-toggle.

## philosophical anchors

- **Vaclav Smil, *Energy and Civilization*** — power density made visible. the empirical legitimacy for treating gasoline as an honest design choice.
- **Lewis Mumford, *Technics and Civilization*** — eotechnic/paleotechnic/neotechnic. the clock as keystone, not the steam engine.
- **Ivan Illich, *Tools for Conviviality*** — repairable, user-comprehensible, anti-surveillance.
- **Donella Meadows, "Leverage Points"** — paradigm changes vs parameter changes. our manifesto's punch lands here.
- **Kevin Kelly, *What Technology Wants*** — deliberate counter-selection pressure in the technium's drift.
- **W. Brian Arthur, *The Nature of Technology*** — combinatorial evolution of how technologies compose.

we explicitly avoid: Baudrillard, Derrida, Bifo, Land. the project's gravity is empirical-systems, not po-mo semiotic.

## historical foils — what we celebrate

- **Cox Tee Dee .049** — 1948–2006 in production at cox. the most-built model glow engine in history. our mk i engine.
- **TA-312 field telephone** — magneto-cranked, bakelite, canvas-bagged. our case aesthetic.
- **HP 200LX** — 1994 palmtop. real keyboard, AA batteries, ran for weeks. battery ethic.
- **Olivetti Lettera 32** — when industrial design was its own argument.
- **Petromax 821** — pressure-kerosene mantle lantern. our brass-and-pyrex sensibility.

## historical foils — what we learn from failure

- **Toshiba Dynario** — 2009 DMFC USB charger. 3,000-unit limited run. the only consumer fuel-cell device that shipped. then nothing.
- **Lilliputian Systems Nectar** — 2013 butane SOFC. raised $150M. never shipped.
- **eZelleron Kraftwerk** — 2015 dresden kickstarter. butane SOFC. collapsed.
- **Intelligent Energy Upp** — 2014 PEM H₂ at Apple Stores. discontinued 2017.
- **Epiphany onE Puck** — 2014 hot-drink Stirling charger kickstarter. collapsed.

the lesson: the entire consumer electronics industry tried to ship a fuel-powered phone from 2001 to 2017 and every single product failed in market. we are not arguing that they were right. we are arguing that the loss was real.
