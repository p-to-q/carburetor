## the one-line claim

a phone whose energy source is fuel, not battery, is honest about power in a way a battery-phone cannot be.

## the four beats this essay must hit

### beat 1 — the lithium-ion default is opaque

- batteries hide energy as a calendar abstraction. cycles count, capacity fades, none of it visible.
- the user has no analog mental model of "how much" is "in" the device.
- charging is invisible at home. a flat battery is a panic away from civilization.
- the carbon cost is real but not perceptible. the device feels like it runs on "nothing."

### beat 2 — fuel makes energy a substance you can perceive

- you pour it. you smell it. you spill it. you watch it run out.
- the warm-up is a feature: the device tells you it is preparing.
- "battery anxiety" is replaced by "fuel readiness" — a different relationship to autonomy.
- the device weighs more on purpose. weight is information.

### beat 3 — this is not "going back". this is fixing a layering mistake.

- citation: Vaclav Smil, *Energy and Civilization* — power density made visible.
- citation: Lewis Mumford, *Technics and Civilization* — eotechnic / paleotechnic / neotechnic framing.
- citation: Ivan Illich, *Tools for Conviviality* — the language to argue a gasoline phone is more *convivial* than a smartphone.
- citation: Donella Meadows, "Leverage Points" — paradigm changes vs parameter changes; this essay's punch lands here.
- the claim: separating energy from compute lets each be honest about what it is. modern compute is fast and slow-degrading; modern energy storage is slow and fast-degrading. our default has collapsed them into one part. uncollapsing is engineering, not nostalgia.

### beat 4 — we built it. here is what we measured.

- pointer to `Receipts (not claims)` in `README.md`.
- pointer to `docs/architecture.md`.
- pointer to `docs/editions.md` — three sincere ways to honor the design.
- the closing triad (see below).

- it must not name-drop Baudrillard, Derrida, Bifo, or Land.
- it must not use the word "literally" (unless meaning is literal mechanical).
- it must not use "let's" or "just" softeners.
