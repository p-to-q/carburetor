## page 27 (right, three short paragraphs)

```
VAPORE — the simulator

zero dollars. open a browser. pour fuel, prime, crank,
listen for the engine note, watch the supercap bus
climb, send a text, run out of fuel. vapore implements
all five layers in software; the telemetry frames it
produces are byte-identical to hardware. it is not a
toy. it is the architecture's first-class implementation.

PROCESSTA — the maker

~$120, one weekend, parts from a single aliexpress order.
an esp32-s3 with a built-in display, a generic .15
nitro engine, a tp4056 charging module, a tool-pouch
case. no cellular modem; the phone in your pocket does
the cellular work over BLE. processta is more honest
with the "you cannot call on it" rule than classica —
it cannot even pretend.

CLASSICA — the definitive

~$760. several weekends. every part curated for ideal
behavior in its layer. the cox engine sits at the
project's spec; the sharp memory lcd is the canonical
low-power reflective display; the q10 keyboard is the
best thumb keyboard ever shipped. classica is closer
to a leica purchase than a smartphone purchase.
```

set in Plex Serif 10pt / 14pt. each paragraph's title in Plex Sans Bold 14pt brass.

## the small print at the bottom of page 27

and a $760 version against the same interfaces, the
