# edizione spec

locked at v0.1. soft-list at bottom.

## trim & binding

- trim size: 148 × 210 mm (A5 portrait)
- page count: 32 interior + 4-page wrap cover
- binding: saddle-stitch, two staples, ~70 mm from the short edge
- spine: not applicable (saddle-stitch lays flat)

## paper

- **interior**: 80 gsm offset uncoated, FSC-certified if available. matte feel.
- **cover**: 120 gsm matte coated, scored for the wrap fold.

## color

- **ink 1**: process black, K-only on interior.
- **ink 2 (spot)**: brass.
  - primary reference: Pantone Metallic 871 C
  - non-metallic fallback: Pantone 4515 C (warm sand) if metallic unavailable
  - cover may use brass foil stamping instead of spot ink — vendor-dependent

## typography

- **body**: IBM Plex Serif, 10pt / 14pt leading.
- **caption / monospace**: IBM Plex Mono, 8pt / 12pt leading.
- **display**: IBM Plex Sans, weights 200–700, sized to context.
- **license**: IBM Plex is OFL (SIL Open Font License) — bundled redistributable.

see `typography.md` for hierarchy and brass-color usage rules.

## margins

- top: 18 mm
- bottom: 18 mm
- inner (toward staples): 16 mm
- outer (toward trim): 14 mm
- bleed: 3 mm on all edges where art runs to trim

## image specifications

- **raster**: 300 dpi at final print size. grayscale + 1 spot, or pure K for line art.
- **vector**: SVG, all text outlined for press, spot color named `BRASS_871`.
- **export**: PDF/X-1a for press delivery. fonts embedded. transparency flattened.

## file delivery

- **interior PDF**: single file, paginated 1–32, ink 1 (K) + ink 2 (`BRASS_871`).
- **cover PDF**: single file, 4-page imposition (front-back wrap). 3 mm bleed.
- **proofs**: separate low-res screen proof for editorial review.

## print run

- edizione I: 100 units
- distribution:

  - 15 archive (Wooden Computer Co., Ltd.)
