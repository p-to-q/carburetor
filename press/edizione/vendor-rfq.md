# vendor RFQ

four printers we will quote. send this as a single email per vendor, with the body below adapted to their preferred format.

## the shortlist

ranked by aesthetic fit (top is best fit for the project's spirit).

### 1. local risograph studio — **first choice if available**

risograph reproduces brass beautifully via a soy-based gold ink. perfect aesthetic match: slightly imperfect registration, organic texture, the print equivalent of a hand-cranked engine.

candidates to RFQ:
- **Risolab Tokyo** (if shipping internationally is acceptable)
- **Outpost Editions** (Brooklyn, NY)
- **Hato Press** (London)
- **People of Print** (London) — wider machine bed
- **Banditrade** (Beijing)
- any local studio with a Riso SF or MZ-class machine

pros: aesthetic perfection; small runs OK; reasonable cost.
cons: limited paper sizes; max ~100 units per session; binding usually outsourced.

### 2. Mixam — **best balance of quality / scale / brass support**

UK/US/CA print-on-demand and short-run. offers Pantone Metallic 871 C as a true spot color. saddle-stitch, A5, 80 gsm uncoated all standard.

quote URL: <https://mixam.com>
estimated at 100 units, 32pp interior + 4pp cover: **~$420 total** (~$4.20/unit).
turnaround: ~10 business days from PDF approval.

pros: vetted, fast, real spot color, online portal.
cons: lacks the Riso aesthetic; cover stock is coated by default (we want matte — confirm in RFQ).

### 3. Newspaper Club — **wildcard option for a tabloid format**

UK tabloid newsprint print-on-demand. **NOT A5.** would require a complete redesign of the spine if chosen. listed here only because the newsprint aesthetic is so strong; would shift the project from "zine" to "broadside".

decision: **fork as an alternate format** if we want both an A5 zine and a newspaper. v0.1 stays A5.

### 4. Lulu Direct — **fallback for international fulfillment**

US POD, no minimum order. brass spot via foil stamp at extra cost (~$1.50/unit).

pros: zero inventory; ships globally; predictable cost.
cons: coated cover only; foil stamp is more expensive than spot; slower per-unit production.

send to: vendor sales contact.

Subject: RFQ — A5 saddle-stitch zine, 100 units, brass spot color

Hello,

We are p-to-q / Wooden Computer Co., Ltd., a small independent
research practice. We are producing the first edition of a printed
publication called "edizione I", described below. We would
appreciate a quote.

JOB SPECIFICATION

Trim:           148 × 210 mm (A5 portrait)
Pages:          32 interior + 4-page wrap cover
Binding:        saddle-stitch, two staples
Paper:          80 gsm offset uncoated interior, FSC-certified
                preferred; 120 gsm matte coated cover
Inks:           Process Black (K-only) on interior;
                Process Black + 1 spot color on cover.
                Spot color: Pantone Metallic 871 C.
                Foil stamp acceptable if Pantone Metallic spot
                is unavailable.
Quantity:       100 units
Files:          We will deliver PDF/X-1a with all fonts
                embedded and transparency flattened.
                Bleed: 3 mm on all edges.

QUESTIONS

  1. Cost per unit at 100 units, plus setup if applicable.
  2. Cost per unit at 200 units (stretch) for comparison.
  3. Lead time from PDF approval to in-hand.
  4. Shipping cost to (USER'S ADDRESS).
  5. Whether you can supply a physical proof before the production run.
  6. Any constraints on the brass spot color we should be aware of.

We are willing to choose paper or finish substitutes if they preserve
the aesthetic intent (matte, uncoated interior, real brass on cover).

The publication is non-commercial: sold at cost via our website,
with any surplus returned to the next print run.

Please reply by (USER + 2 weeks). Happy to send sample pages
or the production PDF on request.

— Wooden Computer Co., Ltd.
hi@ptoq.io
