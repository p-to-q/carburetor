# 00-frontispiece · page 1

a full-bleed reproduction of `../../design/exploded-view-mk1.svg`, sized to fit the trim area minus a 14 mm margin on all sides.

callouts 01–12 are inked in brass. all leader lines and labels are inked in K (black).

at the bottom-outside corner, a small caption in Plex Mono 7pt:

> mk i · field · 12 parts · 650 g loaded

## build pipeline note

the build script `build-scripts/build.mjs` reads the SVG from `../../design/exploded-view-mk1.svg`, resizes it to A5-minus-margins (148-14×2 × 210-14-18 = 120 × 178 mm), and embeds it as inline SVG on this page. no PNG conversion; vector all the way through.

## what this page is for

a frontispiece is a promise. it tells the reader, before any prose: this is a thing you can take apart. every piece has a name. nothing is hidden.

`Q.E.D.`
