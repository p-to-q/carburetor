## the four rules

a manifesto for this project must not:

1. **apologize for the strangeness.** the project is unusual on purpose.
2. **use "literally," "let's," "just" softeners, or hype copy.** the user's register will roll its eyes.
3. **name-drop philosophers without making them do work.** if Smil's name appears, his argument must too.
4. **exceed ~600 words across the four pages.** longer manifestos stop being read.

## the ratio that holds the spread together

ink : whitespace ≈ 1 : 1. count the printed glyphs and stop yourself when you're at the page's halfway-by-area.
