## the discipline

page 32 is half breath, half declaration. resist the temptation to add anything else here. no QR code. no last-minute promo. no "thank you for reading."

if a reader reaches page 32 and sees only Q.E.D. and a small legal block and a serial number, they have read the booklet correctly.
